## Fact-check notes that shaped the wording

- Six metres is the thickness of a deposit that contains ash and charcoal, not a heap of pure ash from one fire, hence "a layer with ash and charcoal"; the hearths in the section are separate lenses.
- Friction gives a smouldering ember, not a flame: the ember is put into dry grass and only then does a flame appear.
- Working sparks come from flint struck against pyrite (flint on flint gives no usable spark); the tinder smoulders first.
- The animal is a hyena: it lived both in Africa and near the cave by Beijing. Its eyes shine with reflected firelight.
