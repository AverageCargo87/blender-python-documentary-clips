# Narration, English translation: Kostenki, mammoth hunters

Translation of [narration.ru.md](narration.ru.md) for readers of this repository. The clip itself is narrated in Russian.

| id | start, s | text |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | 1.0 | Kostenki. Camps of mammoth hunters. |
| S2 | 6.8 | Voronezh Region, the high bank of the Don. |
| S3 | 12.6 | People of the modern type lived here for many thousands of years. The oldest camps are about forty-five thousand years old. |
| S4 | 22.6 | How did scientists find out the age of the finds? The deeper a layer of earth lies, the older it is. |
| S5 | 33.4 | Here is a thin light layer: ash from a volcano in Italy. It fell about forty thousand years ago. So everything that lies below it is even older. |
| S6 | 45.2 | Much later, about twenty-four thousand years ago, the mammoth hunters built dwellings out of mammoth bones. |
| S7 | 56.0 | This is how such a dwelling was built. A ring of bones nine metres across. The base of the wall is mammoth skulls, and the posts are long bones. |
| S8 | 66.6 | A fire was kept burning inside. And on top there was a roof of hides. |
| S9 | 74.8 | The hunters made tools from mammoth tusk, bone and flint: points, needles, awls. And also ornaments: beads and pendants. |
| S10 | 86.4 | Female figurines carved from mammoth tusk were found here too. One of them, the Venus of Kostenki, was carved more than twenty-one thousand years ago. |
| S11 | 96.6 | Such figurines are kept in museums in Saint Petersburg. |
| S12 | 102.6 | Today the excavation site is the Kostenki museum reserve. More than twenty camps have been found here. |
| S13 | 109.8 | The museum building stands right over an ancient dwelling made of mammoth bones. |
