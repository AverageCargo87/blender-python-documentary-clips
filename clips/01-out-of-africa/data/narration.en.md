# Narration, English translation: Out of Africa

Translation of [narration.ru.md](narration.ru.md) for readers of this repository. The clip itself is narrated in Russian.

| id | start, s | text |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | 5.8 | The ancestral home of humankind is Africa. |
| S2 | 8.4 | In the south of Africa, remains of Australopithecus were found: the "southern ape", a predecessor of the first humans. |
| S3 | 15.6 | About two and a half million years ago Homo habilis, "handy man", appeared; his remains were found in Olduvai Gorge in East Africa. |
| S4 | 25.1 | About a million years ago Homo erectus, "upright man", appeared: his remains were found far from Africa, on the island of Java, and the remains of Sinanthropus in China. |
| S5 | 39.0 | In Europe, Neanderthals lived from three hundred to forty thousand years ago. |
| S6 | 46.0 | And the earliest people of the modern type formed in Africa and came to Europe more than forty thousand years ago. |
| S7 | 57.6 | Their remains have been found in every part of the world. |
| S8 | 61.0 | In the end, only one human species remained on Earth: ours. |
